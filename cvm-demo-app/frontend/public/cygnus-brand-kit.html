Usage guidelines

Clear space

Maintain a minimum clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of the core circle. The mark needs room to breathe — especially at small sizes.

Background rules

Use the dark variant on navy or dark backgrounds. Use the light variant on white or off-white backgrounds. Never place the mark on busy images or patterns.

Mark alone is fine

The accretion disk mark works standalone (without "CYGNUS" wordmark) for favicons, app icons, loading states, and inline references. It's designed to be recognizable at 16px.

With "Ricardo Uemura"

When the consultant brand and product brand appear together: "Ricardo Uemura" in editorial context, then "powered by Cygnus" or "built with Cygnus" in IBM Plex Sans + blue accent.

Don't rotate or skew

The ellipses represent a specific viewing angle of the accretion disk. Rotating the mark breaks the metaphor and looks like a generic target.

Don't change the blue

Signal Blue (#1e90ff) is the core brand color. Don't substitute with other blues, gradients, or color effects. The opacity layers in the mark are carefully calibrated.
